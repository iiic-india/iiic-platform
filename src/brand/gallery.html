The rule

Yellow and a dark surface together read as a hazard sign. So yellow only ever sits on paper, white, or its own tints; it is a fill or the bar, never text and never a thin line; and dark tones are reserved for type and the illustration.

Status colours are for alerts only. Green and red never appear in marketing surfaces; caution uses the yellow family.
02 / Logo

The lockup is untouched. Here is where it goes.

SVG masters in logo/, PNG exports in logo/png. The yellow bar is part of the drawing; the kit borrows it as a device but never redraws the logo.

Spacing and size

Clear space equals the bar's width on every side. Minimum height: lockup 28px on screen, 12mm in print; mark 20px on screen.

The small marks

Below 48px the face cannot be read, so the favicon is a geometric lowercase i on the yellow square (SVG, PNG and a multi-size favicon.ico). From 180px up the face returns. The maskable icon keeps the face inside the 80% safe circle.
