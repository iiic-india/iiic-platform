It helps you understand how you operate so your potential becomes a matter of choice, not circumstance." (SSB). Basis: "concepts of self through Shrimad Bhagwat Gita, Psychology & Sociology" (GB). Who it is for: "Entrepreneurs, business owners, coaches, consultants, professionals, homemakers, or anyone who wants to live by conscious choice rather than emotional reaction. It is not age based. It is awareness based." (SSB). What changes: take decisions with clear purpose; use logical reasoning over emotional impulse; build discipline, accountability and decisiveness; recognise the true self beyond external influence; align personal and professional goals (SSB).

Business & Management Consultancy, "Leadership, Systems & Culture": leadership development, and systems, processes and SOPs "through an HR-centric approach", resting on three pillars, people, processes and culture. Counselling, "Perspective, Acceptance & Growth": perspective, acceptance and self-understanding. The path of change: "Awareness → Mindset Shift → Informed Action → Sustainable Growth". The description's earlier outcome lines are kept on file and no longer used (Counselling: "Self-awareness, clarity and personal accountability"; Consultancy: "Decisiveness, discipline and accountability in business and leadership"; Education: "a strong sense of purpose, independent thinking, self-worth, discipline and accountability"); its five education points (Purpose, Identity, Significance, Logic, Accountability) stay in use.
- Consultancy services: organisation planning; marketing and sales; brand positioning (IIIC's brief and the site). Target: small and mid-size non-e-commerce businesses; founders and HR leads (IIIC's brief). The founders' page addresses "founders, business owners and SME/MSME leaders". The GB lists seven corporate services and seven for colleges and universities, under different names (I-11).

The form also lists fees and four time windows: IIIC has decided not to publish prices for now (D-21) and the platform books from the counsellor's real calendar instead of fixed windows (D-02), so neither appears on any page, email or template.

- University work: "Subjective Self; 2 Days Workshop On Self Knowledge", Pandit Deendayal Energy University, 3–4 April 2023 (site). The site attributes its testimonials to "students of SLS, Pandit Deendayal Energy University, Gandhinagar"; the expansion of "SLS" is confirmed in I-31.
