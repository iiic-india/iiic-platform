# 17 · The pipeline: the design (M4)

The design M4 is built to: the data, the stages, how a request becomes a lead, the proposal's life, the public forms, the events, and who builds what. Written 23 November 2026 on branch `m4/foundation`, from `m3/core` (86929b8), which holds the collections, their access rules, the contracts in `src/lib/pipeline`, the forms' validation and the first tests. The target is M4's "Done when" (`10`): a Team Map request creates a lead with an operator task; a proposal can be generated, previewed, sent and tracked; stale leads surface. Sources: `01` §5, `02` (`/organisations/team-map`, `/about`, `/institute`), `03` F3, F5, F12, `04` (People and organisations, B2B), `05` (Pipeline, Today, key workflows, D-78), `06` (P1–P7), `08` §3, §5, §9, `13` §3–§4, `15` §3.6, D-09, D-21, D-24, D-35, D-78, and `16` for the patterns M3 set. Where this document and those differ, they win, and this one is corrected.

## 1. Rules this design keeps

- Nothing is invented: every stage name is the docs'; every proposal word, fee, term and validity is typed by IIIC's staff; nothing on the boards, the forms or a proposal is a default about IIIC (CLAUDE.md #1). No price on a page, email or template while `prices_public` is off (D-21, §5).
- Consent before storage: a form's consent is recorded under the notice version the person saw, in the same transaction as everything it covers, and nothing is stored unless all of it is (CLAUDE.md #4). One unticked box; no pre-ticked or bundled consent; no false urgency (#9).
- Every custom view, server function and route checks the user, the role and the two-factor code first (#6); every write is in the audit log (the plugin covers every new collection). Events carry ids only; logs hold no name, contact detail or note. Times in UTC, shown in India; phones in E.164 (#8). Typed `Result`s (`src/lib/result.ts`), Zod at every boundary, `server-only` for anything with secrets or the database outside the collections' hooks.
- Leads are S1 (business), enquiries S2 (they hold contact details as typed), as `04` and `08` §2 class them. Nothing from counselling belongs in a lead, a note or a proposal (#3).

## 3. A lead's stages

Lists and moves live in `src/lib/pipeline/stages.ts`; the select, the board's columns and the tests are built from them.

- **Consultancy** (`03` F5, `05`): New → Contacted → Team Map scheduled → Team Map done → Debrief held → Proposal sent → Won | Lost (with why).
- **Partnership** (`03` F12): New → In conversation → MoU drafted → Signed | Closed (with why; proposed, §10).
- **Moves** (proposed): an active stage moves to any other stage of its own board, forwards, backwards (to put a slip right) or to an end; an end moves nowhere. A repeat request after an end opens a new lead that names the old one (`previousLead`). A lead's stage stays on its own board (field check). Every move is a `stageChange` activity and emits `lead.stageChanged`.
- **The platform's own moves.** A proposal call booked (P2) brings a consultancy lead to Contacted if it is earlier; a proposal sent (P5) to Proposal sent if earlier. Nothing else moves a lead on its own.

## 4. From a request to a lead

`ReceiveEnquiry` (`src/lib/pipeline/leads.ts`, builder A) runs in one transaction, in this order, and stores nothing unless it stores all of it:

1. **The person.** Matched by email (small letters), else by mobile (E.164); if the two point at different people, the email wins and the enquiry says so for the office (`conflict`). A match is linked, never changed: an unverified form must not rewrite a record. No match: a person is made with the name, email, mobile, organisation and role as typed, source "Team Map request" or "Partnership enquiry", and the UTM.
2. **The consent** for `enquiryReplies` under the notice version the form showed, with the form's evidence (IP, browser, form name), recorded through the ledger (`src/collections/Consents.ts` checks the notice was in force). If that version is no longer the one in force, the action answers `notice-changed` and the form asks again with the new text.
3. **The organisation.** Matched by its website's host (no "www."), else by the work email's domain unless it is a free mail service (`FREE_MAIL_DOMAINS`), else by its name folded (small letters, "&" as "and", punctuation and a trailing legal form such as Pvt Ltd dropped), of the same type (business for a Team Map, institution for a partnership). No match: one is made from the form. A match is never changed.
4. **The enquiry**, with the fields as typed, the consent's id, the page, Turnstile's outcome and the submitter hash.
5. **The lead.** The organisation's open lead of the same kind, else the person's, is matched: the enquiry is added to it (outcome "added to an open lead"). Otherwise a new lead in New, source Team Map request or partnership enquiry, its title from the organisation.
6. **The timeline and the task.** A "request received" activity on the lead; and, unless the lead already has an open first-reply task, a task for the operator, "Reply to the request", due in two working days (P1, P7; key `P1:enquiry:<id>` or `P7:…`). A working day is Monday to Friday in India (the office's hours); holidays are an input (§10), none until given.
7. **Events:** `enquiry.received`, and `lead.created` and `task.created` for what was made.

An enquiry the office marks as spam stays (for the audit), is left off the boards' counts, and its lead, if the enquiry made it and no other request that is not spam has joined it since, is closed as Lost or Closed with the reason "Spam" by the office (the enquiries' hook, as the office's move). Duplicate people and organisations that matching missed are merged by the office in M5 (`15` §3.6).

## 5. A proposal's life

**Draft and versions.** The office opens "Create proposal" on a lead: a `proposals` record in draft with a reference. Its parts are the kit's template's (`print/proposal.html`): title, subtitle, prepared by, situation, approach, phases, measures, fees and next step, each typed by staff; an owner types the fees (operators change no price, `05`); the platform adds only the reference, the date, the client's name (the organisation's) and the total, worked out on the server. "Create version" freezes the working copy as version 1, 2, 3 (`ProposalSnapshot` in `document`), refused as `incomplete` while a part the template prints is empty; a later version moves a sent, viewed or expired proposal back to draft, and the previous version's link closes when the new one is sent.

**Statuses** (`PROPOSAL_MOVES`): draft → sent → viewed → accepted | declined | expired; sent, viewed and expired go back to draft when revised; accepted and declined are final. Accepted and declined are what the client said, recorded by the office with a note: the template's "Accepted for … Name, title, date" is signed outside the platform, so there is no accept button on the link (proposed). Expired: a sent or viewed proposal past its "valid until" day (only when the office gave one; no default validity), by the job `P8-expire-proposals` (P8 proposed) or when the proposal is next read.

**The PDF per version** is printed from `document` through the documents slice's renderer (`renderProposalPdf`, D-09, on `m3/documents`), which refuses a page still holding a placeholder; the preview keeps placeholders, for the office. Until the private bucket is wired (`16` §10) the PDF is printed again on each opening and the SHA-256 of the copy sent is kept; then the sent copy is stored and its key recorded, and that copy is what the link serves.

**Fees and D-21.** A proposal prints its fees, and D-21 keeps prices off every page, email and template while `prices_public` is off. Whether a quote to one organisation stands outside D-21, as a payment link's amount does (D-24), is IIIC's to rule (proposed, §10). Until then `MayPrintFees` answers `pricesPublic`: with prices hidden, a version can be made and its working copy read in the admin, but its preview page and its PDF are refused (`fees-hidden`), so nothing is sent. The tests run with the flag on and fake fees. The proposal email never carries an amount, whatever the ruling.

**Sending** (`SendVersion`, owner or operator with the code). One transaction: print, record the hash, issue the tracked link (32 random bytes, base64url; only its SHA-256 kept), close any earlier version's link, set the proposal sent, move the lead (§3), log a "proposal" activity, make the follow-up task due in three working days (P5, key `P5:version:<id>`), emit `proposal.sent`. After it commits: email the link to the lead's contact through Payload's mail adapter (Resend once configured, `13`; plain words, the reference in the subject, no amount; M6 swaps in the React Email template and the message log) and record `emailedAt`. The token exists only in that call, so no job sends it; if the mail fails, the proposal stays sent, the office sees the link once to copy into its own message, or sends again, which issues a new link and closes the old.

**Tracking.** `/p/[token]` (noindex, `no-store`, no referrer, never in the sitemap) shows the proposal's title and client and a button to open the PDF; the PDF's own address counts the opening (first and last opened, times opened), so link scanners that only fetch the page count nothing. The first opening moves a sent proposal to viewed and emits `proposal.viewed`. A replaced, withdrawn, declined or expired version's link answers with one neutral page that says the link no longer opens a proposal and how to reach IIIC, for every case alike; an accepted one stays open. Tokens are compared by hash; an unknown token gets the same page.

## 6. The public forms

Built as typed server actions, not the form-builder plugin (OS-5), as fixed page blocks with no fields, as the contact and scope blocks are: `teamMapRequest` (id `team-map-request`) and `partnershipEnquiry` (id `partnership-enquiry`). A CMS page places one; the starter pages place them on `/organisations/team-map` (in the request section's place, where the hero's "Request a Team Map" leads) and `/institute` (the Partnerships section); `/about`'s and `/learn`'s "Partner with IIIC" lead to it, and a later `/institute/partnerships` page can carry the same block.

| | Team Map request (F5) | Partnership enquiry (F12) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fields | company*, website, team size*, sector*, your name*, your role*, work email*, mobile*, what prompted the request | institution*, your name*, your role*, email*, phone*, what the partnership would be about* |
| Consent | one unticked box for `enquiryReplies`, with the notice text in force beside it and a link to the privacy notice | the same |
| Afterwards | the form is replaced by an acknowledgement that takes focus: the request reached IIIC, the office replies within two working days (`13` §4), and the proposal call can be booked meanwhile | the same, without the call |

- **Validation:** `src/lib/pipeline/forms.ts` (Zod; tests `tests/unit/pipelineForms.spec.ts`): text trimmed and length-limited, email in small letters, phone in E.164 (an Indian number without its code), website in one form (http or https, a real host, no port or password), the team-size band from its list, the box ticked (`consent: 'yes'`), the notice version a positive whole number. Errors return to the form with the kit's error summary, focus moved to it, each message beside its field; what was typed stays.
- **No notice, no form.** While no consent text for `enquiryReplies` is in force (`currentNotice`), the block shows a placeholder (`[[TODO: the enquiry consent text, I-22]]`) instead of the form, and the action refuses (`no-notice`).
- **Spam, without keys:** a trap field hidden from people and screen readers, and a signed time the form was drawn (an HMAC under the server's secret); a submission with the trap filled or sent within three seconds gets the acknowledgement and stores nothing. **With keys:** Turnstile's widget on the form and its token checked on the server (`siteverify`, with the address); a failed check asks the person to try again. Turnstile's origin joins the CSP only where its keys are set.
- **Rate limits:** at most 5 enquiries an hour from one network address (counted by `submitterHash`, an HMAC of the address under a key of the day, derived from the server's secret as the analytics session hash is; the address itself is never stored) and 3 a day from one email address; past either, the form says it already has their requests and gives the office's email. Vercel's firewall rule on the forms comes in M7 (`12` §5).
- **Analytics:** `request_submitted` with the route and register only (`12` §7).
- **As built (B).** The order on the server is the trap and the signed time (a robot gets the acknowledgement), the notice in force, the fields, Turnstile, the limits, then `ReceiveEnquiry` (`src/lib/enquiryForms/submit.ts`, a pure core the tests give fakes; `actions.ts` holds the two server actions). The forms post to their page with no script (`useActionState` with the page's address and the form's id), and the browser checks nothing (`noValidate`), so the summary is the same either way. A page reads its forms' setup (the notice, the signed time, Turnstile's site key, the action) only when it carries a form block (`pageForms.ts`, which loads `setup.ts` on demand). On `/institute` the form follows the Partnerships section, whose Contact button gives way to it; the hero keeps Contact. The forms' words carry the candidate marker (I-41), and the placeholders report lists each form's section as a candidate and its I-22 placeholder while no notice is in force. Turnstile's origin joins `script-src` and `frame-src` (`frame-src` gives up `'none'` for it) on public pages only.

## 7. Stale and untouched leads

Worked out from a lead's own fields when it is read (`HeatOf`, `StaleWhere` in `src/lib/pipeline/staleness.ts`), so they show at once, without a job: **untouched** while in New with no staff activity (`touchedAt` empty), amber from 24 hours after it was made, red from 48 (P3); **stale** in an active stage other than New once its last activity (else its stage change, else its making) is seven days old (P4). The board marks both in words and the kit's badges, and opens with a "Needs attention" list of them; the leads list can be filtered the same way. The job `P3-P4-lead-alerts` (A; run hourly by M6's cron, by an owner through the jobs API until then) raises `lead.untouched` at amber and again at red (a lead it first sees past 48 hours, at red only), and `lead.stale` once per quiet spell, recording each in the lead's `alerts`, so Today (M5) and the digest (M6) can use them.

## 8. Events for M5 and M6

`src/lib/pipeline/events.ts`: ids and codes only, queued inside the change's transaction with an idempotency key (`<event>:<subject>`), as M3's are (`16` §9). `enquiry.received` (P1, P7: the acknowledgement), `lead.created`, `lead.stageChanged` (keyed by its activity), `lead.untouched` (P3, keyed by level), `lead.stale` (P4, keyed by the quiet spell), `task.created` (Today), `proposal.versionCreated`, `proposal.sent` (P5; P6 counts five working days from it), `proposal.viewed`, `proposal.accepted`, `proposal.declined`, `proposal.expired`. M4's own subscription: M3's `booking.confirmed` queues `P2-lead-from-call` (A), added to `LISTENERS` in `src/lib/booking/emit.ts` at the merge. Every message (P1 and P7's acknowledgements, P6's drafted follow-up, the digest) is M6's.

## 9. Retention and erasure

Organisations: seven years after their last invoice (`04`). Leads, their activities and tasks, and enquiries: three years after the lead's last activity (proposed, as `people`; lawyer to confirm, I-22). Proposals and versions: with their lead; an accepted one eight years, as the accounts it underlies (proposed; CA to confirm). Erasing a person deletes their enquiries (the copies of what they typed) and their consents are withdrawn as today; leads and versions keep no link to them (`person` and `sentTo` emptied); the office reviews the lead's notes as part of the request (`05` Privacy, M5). The retention jobs are M6's (O-series).
