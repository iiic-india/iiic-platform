*Indian Institute of Image Catalysts (IIIC)*

_One Psychological Framework. Three Applications. One Purpose: Human Development._

IIIC is an organisation that has developed what it describes as India's first indigenous theory of psychology, known as Subjective Self.

Subjective Self is a psychological framework that encourages individuals to look at themselves as a subject rather than merely as an object of their circumstances. It focuses on understanding oneself, examining one's thought processes, recognising one's role in different situations and taking ownership of one's decisions and actions.

At IIIC, Subjective Self is not limited to one area of application. It is the core psychological philosophy that drives all three of our service verticals:

*1.* *Counselling – Understanding the Self*

Our counselling approach focuses on helping individuals gain clarity about themselves, their circumstances, emotions, thought processes and decisions.

Through the Subjective Self framework, individuals learn to look beyond external circumstances, understand their own role in situations and recognise how their thinking influences their decisions and actions.

Rather than simply providing answers, we help individuals develop the ability to think independently, make conscious decisions and become accountable for their choices.

*Outcome: Self-awareness, clarity and personal accountability.*

*2. Business & Management Consultancy – Developing Better Leaders*

At IIIC, business consultancy goes beyond conventional business advice and management solutions.

We work with business owners, entrepreneurs, leaders and professionals through insightful mentorship rooted in the Subjective Self framework.

Our approach helps individuals understand their approach towards business, identify gaps in their thinking and decision-making, and develop the ability to manage their respective domains with greater clarity.

We focus on enabling leaders to become more decisive in their choices, disciplined in their approach and accountable for their responsibilities and business outcomes.

*Outcome: Decisiveness, discipline and accountability in business and leadership.*

*3. Education – Developing the Human Being*

At IIIC, we believe education should go beyond academic knowledge, conventional learning and preparing individuals to become part of an existing system.

We believe in nurturing human beings who have a strong internal purpose, an independent thought process and a clear understanding of their own significance.

Through the Subjective Self framework, we help individuals understand:

- Purpose: Why they exist beyond simply fulfilling societal expectations.
- Identity: Why developing their individual identity and building their image matters.
- Significance: Why they are important as individuals and what value they can contribute to society.
- Logic: The reasoning behind their choices, actions and responsibilities, rather than simply following instructions.
- Accountability: How to take ownership of their development, decisions and actions.

Our objective is not to produce individuals who simply adapt to an existing system, but to nurture individuals who possess the clarity, reasoning and internal strength to question, understand and contribute meaningfully to society.

We don't just tell individuals what to do; we help them understand why they should do it.

*Outcome: Individuals with a strong sense of purpose, independent thinking, self-worth, discipline and accountability.*
