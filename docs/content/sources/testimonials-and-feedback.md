## B. A longer piece of feedback (supplied 4 November 2026)

Supplied by Yash as "some feedbacks like this"; the writer's name, the programme, the date and whether they consented to publication are not stated (I-37). Verbatim:

> On paper, my life was complete.
>
> A good job. A loving family. Festive gatherings, weekend plans, photographs full of smiles. Everyone around me said I had it all, and I believed them.
>
> Yet every night, when the house fell quiet, one question stayed awake with me.
> Who am I, really?
>
> I searched for the answer everywhere. A new wardrobe. New hobbies. Travel, retreats, a stack of self-help books beside my bed. Each one gave me a few weeks of happiness, and then the feeling faded, leaving me exactly where I began.
>
> I went looking for the right therapist. I sat across from kind, patient people and spoke for hours. The conversations helped me cope, yet I remained in the same place, circling the same questions.
>
> Everything was fine. And still, deep inside, something felt hollow.
>
> That is when I was introduced to Subjective Self.
>
> I walked in expecting one more course. I walked out understanding myself for the first time.
>
> Subjective Self showed me the structure behind my own mind. I began to see how my thoughts took shape, why my emotions rose the way they did, and how many of my choices were echoes of other people's expectations. For years I had been searching outside for something that lived within me all along.
>
> Slowly, the questions found their answers.
>
> I understood my worth, complete in itself, independent of approval, achievement or applause.
> I recognised my true self, beyond the roles I played for everyone else.
> I learnt to observe my emotions, understand them, and choose my response.
>
> Today, my life looks much the same. Same home, same people, same work. The change lives within. The space that once felt hollow is now filled with clarity, with purpose, with me.
>
> Happiness, I realised, was always inside, waiting to be known.

- The copy system's story "The Space Within" appears to be this piece (its last paragraphs name "the space that once felt hollow").
- If this is a real client's own words, it can become a verified story once consent, attribution and date are recorded; if it was written or polished by IIIC, it is an illustrative narrative and must be labelled as one, never shown as a client testimonial (D-28).
