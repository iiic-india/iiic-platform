## 3. Design observations (for `11-experience-concept.md`)

- Both brochures set "SUBJECTIVE SELF" and the Sanskrit line in yellow type on white, and page 3 of the Subjective Self brochure puts yellow on a dark photograph. The brand kit does not allow either (yellow is a fill or the bar, never type; nothing dark next to yellow); the platform renders the same lines in ink on a yellow tile or bar.
- The line-art motif (faces in profile, a brain, a butterfly, geometric solids, a tangle that resolves into a spiral) recurs on both back covers and carries the eight words IIIC uses for its field: learning, counseling, motivation, perception, personality, leadership, behavior, emotion. It is the one illustration style in IIIC's material that suits the kit.
- The brochure logo lock-up matches `src/brand/logo/`.
