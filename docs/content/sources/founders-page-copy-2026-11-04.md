## Notes for use

1. **The story is a narrative, not a case study.** It names no client and claims no result for a named company, so it needs no client approval. Section 2 must not be presented as a verified client story (`08` §6); it is IIIC's illustration of its reader.
2. **"built on over 25 years of research"** matches the general brochure ("established after 25 years of research, application, and refinement"). Keep "25" everywhere unless the founder settles on one wording for I-32 (the current site says "30 years").
3. **"Book a Founder Consultation"** is a new offer name. The platform's consultancy booking is the proposal call (`booking.type = call`, free). Until I-36 says what a Founder Consultation is (length, free or paid, who takes it), the button books a proposal call and keeps this label.
4. **"the Journey Begins Within" / "Begin Your Journey"** are IIIC's words; keep them here. Do not add "journey" elsewhere (VOICE.md, writing standard).
5. **आत्मानं विद्धि** is the settled spelling (D-36, 5 November 2026): the long ā in आत्मानं is the grammatically correct form; the printed brochures' "आत्मनं" is a typo and is not reproduced.
6. The page is written in the masculine ("he holds it up with his own two hands"). That is IIIC's choice for this page; the copy architecture notes where a second version for women founders would be worth writing (a question for IIIC, not a change to this text).
