### The big idea

Every page on the IIIC website tells one story: Subjective Self is the answer, and life offers three places to apply it. A visitor arrives with a question about their life, their business or their child's future, and leaves knowing that every answer begins with understanding the self.

Master line for the website:

> Know Yourself. Every Answer Follows. One Framework. Three Applications. One Purpose: Human Development.

The reader's journey on every page follows four steps, so the feeling of "this has my answers" builds naturally:

1. Recognise: "This is my story." Open with the visitor's own situation in their own words.
2. Understand: "Now I see why." Show the logic of Subjective Self in simple language.
3. Trust: "This is real." Back it with 25+ years of research, Indian philosophical roots and real stories.
4. Act: "I am ready." One clear next step on every page.

Three promises, one root:

| Vertical | The visitor's question | Our promise | Outcome |

| Education | Why do I matter, and where am I going? | Develop the human being | Purpose, presence, wiser decisions |

### Design and messaging rules

These rules keep all three verticals feeling like one institution with one voice.

- One template for every service page. Hero → Does this feel familiar? → Story → Method → What you gain → Who it is for → Outcome band → Call to action. Visitors learn the rhythm once and feel at home on every page.
- Subjective Self appears on every page. A small band near the top of each vertical: Powered by Subjective Self, India's first indigenous theory of psychology, linking to the core page.
- One colour per vertical, one colour for the core. IIIC yellow stays reserved for Subjective Self. Each vertical takes a supporting accent from the brand palette so visitors always know where they are.
- Voice. Warm, premium, authoritative. Short sentences. Positive constructions throughout. No long dashes.
- The question test. Every section answers a question the visitor already carries. When a section answers a question nobody asked, cut it.
- One call to action per page. Repeat it in the hero and the closing band, with the same wording.
- Sanskrit as signature. आत्मानं विद्धि appears once per page, always in the closing band, always in yellow.

## Review against the platform (what the copy architecture keeps, changes or blocks)
