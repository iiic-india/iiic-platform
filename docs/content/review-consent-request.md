# Review messages: asking for a review, asking for consent (D-37, I-42)

Two messages the team sends. The first asks people for a review. The second goes to the writer of each review IIIC wants to publish: it shows them exactly what will appear and lets them choose how their name is shown, or decline. Status: `[proposal]`; the lawyer checks the wording with the rest of the testimonial consent (I-22). Both are also on the intake sheet (`reviews-intake-template.xlsx`, the Request message and Consent message sheets), word for word.

Send both from IIIC's WhatsApp Business number (or by email from an IIIC address), never from a personal number.

## Asking for a review

For people who attended a programme, seminar, workshop, course, consultancy engagement or counselling sessions. It asks for the programme, the year and what changed, which the first replies did not include (`outcomes-map.md`, reading 1).

> Hello [first name], this is [your name] from the Indian Institute of Image Catalysts (IIIC), Gaurang Mistry's institute.
>
> We are collecting a few words from people who have attended his programmes, for IIIC's new website. If you would like to share yours, please reply with:
>
> 1. Your name, as you would like it shown
> 2. Your designation
> 3. Your city
> 4. Which programme, workshop or session you attended, and roughly when
> 5. What changed for you afterwards, in your own words
>
> We will show you exactly how it will appear before anything is published. If you would rather not, there is no need to reply.

Counselling clients: ask only after their sessions have ended, never in a session message or reminder, and add this line before the last paragraph:

> Please leave out anything private about your health or about other people.

Ask open questions only: never suggest what to write, and never offer anything in return. Each reply goes into the intake sheet exactly as written and is screened (copy architecture §6) before anyone is asked for consent.

## Asking for consent

Send it only for reviews that passed the screening tests in `copy-architecture.md` §6.

### Before sending

1. The review is in the intake sheet with its programme, year, where the original is kept, and any connection the writer has to Gaurang Mistry or IIIC; the sheet's "Ready for consent request?" column says Ready.
2. Fill the brackets from the sheet. Paste the review exactly as written. If you propose a shorter excerpt or an English translation, include it in the message so the person approves that too.

### WhatsApp · business and education reviews

> Hello [first name], this is [your name] from the Indian Institute of Image Catalysts (IIIC), Gaurang Mistry's institute.
>
> In [year], after [programme], you wrote:
>
> "[the review, exactly as written]"
>
> We would like to show it on IIIC's new website, like this:
> [name as it would appear] · [designation] · [place]
> [programme], [year]
>
> If your designation or city has changed, tell us what to show.
>
> Please reply with one number:
> 1. Yes, publish it as shown. I still stand by it.
> 2. Yes, but show only my initials.
> 3. Please don't publish it.
>
> You can ask us to remove it at any time by replying here or writing to [email address].

If an excerpt or translation is proposed, add before the numbered choices:

> We would show this part of it: "[excerpt]"
> with this English translation: "[translation]"

If the writer has a connection to disclose (a friend or relative of Gaurang Mistry, or someone who works or worked with IIIC), add after the caption:

> Because [you know Gaurang Mistry personally / you work with IIIC / you worked with IIIC], the card will also say: "[Friend of Gaurang Mistry / Relative of Gaurang Mistry / Works with IIIC / Worked with IIIC]".

### WhatsApp · counselling reviews

Counselling reviews show initials and place unless the person chooses otherwise, so the choices change:

> Please reply with one number:
> 1. Yes, publish it with my initials and city. I still stand by it.
> 2. Yes, publish it with my full name.
> 3. Please don't publish it.

### Email

Subject: May we publish what you wrote about [programme]?

The body is the WhatsApp text above, with "reply with one number" changed to "reply to this email with 1, 2 or 3".

### After the reply

- Record in the intake sheet: the choice (1, 2 or 3), the date, and the file name of the screenshot or email you saved as evidence. Keep the evidence in IIIC's own storage, in the reviews folder.
- No reply means no publication. Send one reminder after five days, then stop.
- A changed designation or city: update the sheet with what the person asked for.
- Someone who asks for removal later: mark it in the sheet the same day; once the platform is live, the withdrawal unpublishes at once (V2).
- A message in Gujarati or Hindi is fine if it says the same things; keep a copy of the exact text sent.
