# Research: Indian legal and compliance requirements

Research brief prepared 30 October 2026. It reports what the sources say; it is **not legal advice**. Every URL was accessed on 30 Oct 2026.
**Conf.:** V = official or primary text read · P = only a reproduction or a reputable secondary source read · U = not confirmed. **L** = a lawyer should confirm.

**Dates that govern the build**
- **In force now:** IT Act s.43A and the SPDI Rules 2011; CERT-In Directions; Consumer Protection Act 2019 and E-Commerce Rules 2020; CCPA guidelines on advertising, coaching ads and dark patterns; GST.
- **13 Nov 2026:** DPDP consent-manager phase (does not affect IIIC).
- **1 Jan 2027:** E-Commerce (Amendment) Rules 2026 take effect.
- **13 May 2027:** all DPDP data fiduciary duties start, and s.43A is omitted (the SPDI Rules lose their legal basis).

**Dark-pattern risk notes** (researcher's reading of the illustrations):
- **Timer or "only X slots left":** False Urgency ("Only 2 rooms left! 30 others are looking at this right now"). Show a timer only for a real hold or real deadline, and never reset it.
- **"Not now" cross-sell card:** JSA's copy of the Trick Question illustration sets "Yes, I would like to receive" against "Not now", and AZB advises against vague options "such as 'not now'". Confirm Shaming: guilt copy ("I will stay unsecured"). Interface Interference: a "light-coloured 'No' option". Nagging: repeated prompts. Basket Sneaking: "Automatic addition of paid ancillary services to the cart". Fix: a plain "No, thanks" with equal visual weight, shown once, never added to the cart automatically.
- **Pre-ticked consent:** barred by E-Commerce Rule 4(9) and invalid under DPDP s.6(1). Tying newsletter or WhatsApp opt-in to a booking risks Forced Action.
- **Booking flow:** Drip Pricing if GST or fees appear late; Bait and Switch if an advertised slot or price is unavailable; Subscription Trap or SaaS Billing if packages auto-renew.

**Pages:** (1) legal information: the proprietor's legal name and trade name, address, website, customer-care email and phone, GSTIN if registered; (2) grievances: Grievance Officer name, designation, contact; 48-hour acknowledgement and one-month resolution; complaint copy and NCH link (from 1 Jan 2027); DPDP privacy contact and the response period of 90 days or less; (3) privacy policy now (SPDI) plus stand-alone DPDP notices per flow (by 13 May 2027) with language options; (4) a rights-request and consent-withdrawal centre; (5) terms; cancellation and refund policy (symmetric charges, refunds to the original method, published timelines); payment-information page; (6) all-in total with breakup, and prior price beside any discount (from 1 Jan 2027); (7) counselling scope page: non-clinical, no protected titles, crisis numbers, confidentiality and POCSO limits, parental consent; (8) course and certificate page with non-degree and non-affiliation wording, no outcome guarantees; (9) testimonials and case studies with consent, genuine, dated, incentives disclosed, no confidential counselling details; (10) the dark-pattern compliance certificate (from 1 Jan 2027).
